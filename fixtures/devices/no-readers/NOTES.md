# devices/no-readers

No readers at all. Not a hardware case: a test of the mechanism.

Every collection must be declared `not_applicable` with a reason naming the
absence, rather than appearing as an empty list. This is P9 in its purest
form — absence derived from the manifests rather than from an empty result —
and it is the assertion that would fail first if the scope declaration were
ever reduced to a formality.
